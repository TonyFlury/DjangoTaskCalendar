Task connections

Boxes are tasks; diamonds are milestones used by task date rules. Solid arrows show completion prerequisites. Dashed arrows show date relations from milestones or tasks; hover an arrow for its day offset. Select a task to edit it below. This preview includes unsaved changes; save the template to keep them.

Negative offsets place tasks above their source. Zero and positive offsets place tasks below it, with date arrows entering at the top. A zero offset still means the same due date. Positions are schematic, not a calendar-day scale. Completion arrows can point back toward an earlier due date when dependencies and dates differ.
