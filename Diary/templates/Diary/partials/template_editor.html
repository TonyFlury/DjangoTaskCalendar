Template navigator help

Editing and saving

Select a task box to open its editor. Add task opens a new task. Save task and Save settings accept edits into this page's draft; Save template creates and approves a new version. Unsaved drafts are lost when you leave or reload the page. Existing diaries keep their tasks and dependencies. Template names stay fixed after creation.

Date sources and completion

Tasks and milestones can both supply a due date. Tasks have owners and completion status; milestones are dates supplied by your site when a diary is created. Choose a date source with a signed offset, including zero, or an annual date. Date sources fix due dates; completion prerequisites identify tasks that must finish first. Later source changes or late completion do not move calculated dates.

Connections and positioning

Boxes are tasks; diamonds are milestone dates. Solid arrows show completion prerequisites; dashed arrows show date sources. Hover a date arrow for its offset. A solid completion arrow replaces a date arrow between the same tasks.

Negative offsets place tasks above their source; zero and positive offsets place tasks below it, with date arrows entering at the top. Zero means the same due date. Positions are schematic, not a calendar-day scale. Completion arrows can point back toward earlier due dates. The layout reduces crossings but complex graphs can still have them.

Zoom, navigation and accessibility

Use + and − to zoom or Fit to screen to show the complete graph. Drag or scroll inside the navigator to pan. Zoom changes the contents, not the panel size. Tab to a task and press Enter to edit. On the graph background, use +, −, or Home to zoom or fit; arrow keys scroll.

Use Find tasks / restore removed tasks below the graph to search by name or restore a removed task. Cancel or Escape discards current dialog edits. Without JavaScript or dialog support, use the inline forms.
