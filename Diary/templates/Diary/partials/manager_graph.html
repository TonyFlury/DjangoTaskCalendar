Graph legend and help

  • ✓ Completed: completion takes priority over past due dates.
  • Late: open and due today or earlier. Blocked tasks retain their blocked label and dashed outline.
  • Blocked · late prerequisite: not itself late, but a direct prerequisite is late.
  • Blocked: waiting for completion prerequisites.
  • Ready: no outstanding completion prerequisite.

Owner needs review and Date not set are separate warnings. Milestones are neutral diamonds, not completable tasks. Show completed uses the same filter as List; Graph does not enable it automatically.

Solid arrows show completion prerequisites; dashed arrows show date sources. Hover/focus a relationship for its meaning. A completion arrow replaces a duplicate date arrow, but describes both relationships. Filtered neighbours are not drawn and no shortcut dependencies are invented.
